About

Strategy, story and craft, each pulling its weight.

I lead design at Xyric.AI, where I shape brand direction, digital experience and the creative systems behind AI products. Before that I led branding and campaigns at Xeven Solutions, YouthClub and Dairaah Ad Agency, across visual identity, social strategy and art direction.

I've also spent much of that time mentoring junior designers in agencies and startups. I'm drawn to identity work that earns its concept, where the idea holds up on a can, a billboard and a phone screen.

Balencia

Balencia is a wellbeing app for real weeks. The identity grows from one line, life at your fingertips: three rising fingertips with Cia, the guide, balanced on top. I designed the mark and its construction, a paper, ink and orange palette, ten 3D characters called the Balens, the five-tab app, the website and an outdoor campaign, all running on one shared design system.

Case study 02 / 11

Xyric

Xyric helps businesses turn data into decisions. The brief came down to one line: businesses don't have a data problem, they have a decision problem. The mark fuses an X with a network of connected nodes. A kinetic palette of electric blue and lime, a typeface system and a node-based visual language carry it from office signage and stationery to merchandise and social.
