About

I’m Agha Noman.

I’m a brand designer based in Lahore, working with clients in Pakistan and the UAE. Over the past five years, I’ve designed for food, retail, fashion and technology brands.

As Design Lead at Xyric.AI, I shape brand direction and digital experiences. My agency and startup work has also included art direction, campaign design and mentoring junior designers.
